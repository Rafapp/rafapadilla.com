## Ongoing explorations

### Alebrije {#alebrije}

A cross-vendor Vulkan/Slang GPU path tracer, and the demo renderer for HiPR. Megakernel compute-shader design on Vulkan inline ray queries (hardware BVH), bindless textures, and OpenPBR materials; next-event estimation with power-heuristic MIS, alias-method light sampling, and Russian roulette. Tiled HiPR scheduling is in progress.

*Source release pending HiPR publication.*
